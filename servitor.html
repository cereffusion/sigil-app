The Servitor

a thought-form with one job — chartered, given form, sealed, and fed
✦ Your servitors
I The Charter every field filled, or it will be unpredictable
1. One narrow job — several small servitors beat one godlike one.
2. Always an off-switch — a lifespan or end condition is mandatory.
3. Consistent then taper — feed often early, then occasional top-ups.
4. Feed, then let go — obsessing over the result starves it.
II Its Form what you will picture when you feed it
the form

The form is drawn from the name and job — the same charter always births the same creature. Reroll shifts its aspect; clicking a tile in "show all" chooses that archetype directly.

III Its Seal the glyph you focus on when feeding
the seal

Drawn on the rose wheel, as in the Forge. For an ornate version with effect inks, open it in the Sigil Forge and it will arrive pre-loaded.

IV The Rituals read with your charter woven in

Birth — once, when the charter is complete

  1. Sit with the seal before you. Remove distractions.
  2. Reach a peak state — deep relaxation, breathwork, or the far side of exertion.
  3. Stare at the seal. Picture the form vividly, doing its one job.
  4. Say aloud: its name, its job, its limits.
  5. Feel the shift from "I am imagining this" to "it exists, apart from me."
  6. Close: "You are —. Your work is —. Go and do it."

Feeding — on the cadence you set

  1. Glance at the seal (or picture the form) for 30–60 seconds.
  2. Picture it actively working.
  3. Say the line once: "—"
  4. Hand it your feed: attention.
  5. Let go. Stop thinking about it until the next feed.

Retirement — when the sign lands or the lifespan ends. Do not skip it.

  1. Bring out the seal.
  2. Say: "—, your work is done. Thank you."
  3. Declare the job complete and the bond released.
  4. Destroy the seal — burn it, or delete it here.
  5. Picture the form unwinding, dissolving back into you.
  6. Close: "You are released. It is finished."
