# Servitor Framework — Charter & Worksheet

A fill-in framework for building, running, and retiring a servitor. Copy the blank
charter, fill every field before you charge it, then use the ritual sequences below.

Companion to [servitor-and-hypersigil-notes.md](servitor-and-hypersigil-notes.md).

---

## 1. The Charter (fill this in first)

> Fill **every** field before charging. Empty fields = an unpredictable servitor.

```
NAME:            ______________________________
  (invented, distinct, easy to say aloud)

ONE-LINE JOB:    ______________________________
  (narrow, present-tense, one task only)

DOMAIN / CATEGORY:  seek / gather / guard / release   (circle one)

FORM (what it looks like):  _____________________
  (creature, shape, light — vivid + consistent)

SEAL:            forged from the NAME  /  from the JOB   (circle one)
  (the glyph you focus on — make it in Sigil Forge)

LIMITS (what it may NOT do):
  - only uses energy I give it
  - does not act on any specific named person
  - ______________________________

FEED METHOD:     ______________________________
  (attention / charged moment / spoken line / offering / breath)

FEED CADENCE:    daily for ____ days, then ____ / week

SPOKEN LINE:     "______________________________"
  (same words every feed)

LIFESPAN / END CONDITION:  ______________________
  (a date, OR "until <goal> happens")

SIGN THE GOAL LANDED:  __________________________
  (the concrete event that means: retire it)
```

---

## 2. The four rules (non-negotiable)

1. **One narrow job.** Several small servitors beat one godlike one.
2. **Always an off-switch.** A lifespan or end condition is mandatory.
3. **Consistent then taper.** Feed often early, then occasional top-ups.
4. **Feed, then let go.** Obsessing over the result starves it.

---

## 3. Birth / charge ritual

Do this once, when the charter is complete.

1. Sit with the seal in front of you. Remove distractions.
2. Reach a peak state — deep relaxation, breathwork, post-exercise, or orgasm.
3. Stare at the seal. Picture the **form** vividly, doing its **job**.
4. Say aloud: its **name**, then its **one-line job**, then its **limits**.
5. Feel the shift from "I'm imagining this" to "it exists, separate from me."
6. Close: "*You are [name]. Your work is [job]. Go and do it.*"

---

## 4. Feed ritual (repeat on cadence)

1. Glance at the seal (or visualize the form) for 30–60 seconds.
2. Picture it actively working — moving toward / guarding / gathering / dissolving.
3. Say the **spoken line** once.
4. Hand it your chosen feed (attention / breath / offering / charged moment).
5. **Let go.** Stop thinking about it until the next feed.

## 5. Retire ritual (license to depart)

Do this when the goal lands OR the lifespan ends. Do not skip it.

1. Bring out the seal.
2. Say: "*[Name], your work is done. Thank you.*"
3. Declare the job complete and the bond released.
4. Destroy the seal — burn it, or erase/delete it.
5. Picture the **form** unwinding, dissolving back into you.
6. Close: "*You are released. It is finished.*"

## 6. Worked example (filled charter)

```
NAME:            Aurelia
ONE-LINE JOB:    help me notice and move toward the right partner
DOMAIN:          seek
FORM:            a warm gold thread that reaches out, finds its match, and draws taut
SEAL:            forged from the NAME ("Aurelia")
LIMITS:          - only uses energy I give it
                 - does not target any specific named person
                 - acts on qualities and openings, not on anyone's free will
FEED METHOD:     attention + spoken line
FEED CADENCE:    daily for 14 days, then 2 / week
SPOKEN LINE:     "Aurelia, find the thread, draw us together, easily."
LIFESPAN:        until I'm in a relationship that feels safe and easy
SIGN IT LANDED:  three months steady with someone who fits the qualities
```

## Quick start (do this now)

1. Copy the blank **Charter** (section 1) into a new note.
2. Fill **NAME** and **ONE-LINE JOB** — the two hardest fields.
3. Forge the **seal** in Sigil Forge; generate the **form** in the form prototype.
4. Fill the rest of the charter.
5. Run the **birth ritual** (section 3) when you're ready.
